Getting around

Welcome to my desktop

This site works like a Windows 7 desktop. Everything about my work lives in windows you can open, move and arrange.

Opening things
  • Double-click an icon on the left to open it.
  • Tap an icon to open it.
  • The EJ button at bottom left opens the Start menu. It lists every window and link, and you can type to search.
  • My Clips opens my published stories at portfolio.edvardi.com.
  • The taskbar shows open windows. Click one to bring it forward, or click the active one to minimize it.
Arranging windows
  • Drag the title bar to move a window. Drag an edge or corner to resize it.
  • Double-click the title bar or use the middle button to maximize.
  • Drag a window to the left or right edge to snap it to half the screen, or to the top edge to maximize it.
